# Q2 — Adaptive music vs the turn clock

**Answer:** Ship cue IDs and overlay triggers to a client mixer (C + A). Vertical reorchestration (B) is a slow, quantized client *state* (district tone), not per-tick stem surgery.

1. **The industry contract is already C: the host names a cue; the player builds a continuous soundtrack from chunks.** IASIG/MMA, 2008 (*Interactive XMF* DRAFT 0.9.1, https://igda-website.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/18181417/ixmf_draft-v091.pdf). Objects are Cue, Chunk, Transition, CueStart; the player owns musical time, sync groups, and mix/mute of parallel tracks. **Implication:** emit cue ids; Motif layering stays in the client pack.

2. **A stinger is an overlay mixed over playing music, not a replacement stem.** Audiokinetic, 2025 (*Using Stingers*, https://www.audiokinetic.com/library/2025.1.7_9143/?source=Help&id=using_stingers): “superimposed and mixed over the currently playing music,” waitable to Next Bar/Cue. FMOD quantizes transitions to tempo markers, not API ticks (*Authoring Events*, 2.02, https://www.fmod.com/docs/2.02/studio/authoring-events.html). **Implication:** A for victory/defeat/retreat; sting-must-not-kill-zone-stem is middleware default.

3. **Vertical layering is an intensity slider inside one piece; horizontal resequencing is a state change — and vertical is a composition puzzle.** Winifred Phillips, 2017 (Mix, https://www.mixonline.com/recording/game-composer-winifred-phillips-429243). Layers “are not simply stems”; each must stand alone; six layers are “exponentially more headache inducing” than three. *Speed Racer* cuts waited for beat markers. **Implication:** B is Motif’s job when district tone is a *state*; do not re-author layer puzzles in the turn engine.

4. **Vertical reorchestration stays musical only under shared tempo, meter, and harmony; waiting for the next bar kills the responsiveness that made vertical attractive.** Denis Zlobin, 2016 (*Adaptive music in competitive games*, https://www.gamedeveloper.com/audio/adaptive-music-in-competitive-games). Quantizing the handoff “reduces its responsivness which is the main advantage.” **Implication:** `intensify`/`soften` on every tick fights the clock; bind them to slow district-tone RTPCs, quantized on the client.

5. **Crossfade-only or immediate cue swaps produce a “musical slide show”; the mixer must own transition type and musical boundary.** Guy Whitmore, 2003 (*Design With Music In Mind*, https://www.gamedeveloper.com/audio/design-with-music-in-mind-a-guide-to-adaptive-audio-for-game-designers). Silence-gaps and crossfades “never” keep momentum; integration is two-way. **Implication:** executing AudioCommands as one-shot mixer ops fails. A-as-*events*, C-as-contract.

6. **Over-adaptive / purely reactive scores fail: they cannot foreshadow, and excess variation unglues the piece.** Andrew Boyd, 2000 (“Pay No Attention to the Orchestra Behind the Curtain!”, *Game Developer*, Sept 2000, https://media.gdcvault.com/GD_Mag_Archives/GDM_September_2000.pdf): so-called interactive music “only really manages to be ‘reactive’ — and is boring to boot.” Karen Collins, 2008 (*Game Sound*, MIT Press, ISBN 978-0-262-03378-7; ch. 8, https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/6284541) lists variable mixing as *one* of ten tools; players switch music *off* when repetition is obvious. **Implication:** forbid per-tick B; silence (or a held drone) is a valid cue.

7. **Adaptive change itself inflates reported tension, even when the mapping is inverted — so over-signaling discrete events will lie.** Cale Plut & Philippe Pasquier, 2019 (*Music Matters*, IEEE CoG, DOI 10.1109/CIG.2019.8847951). Adaptive music raised experienced tension over linear *even when mapped inversely*. **Implication:** no sting or layer bump on every tick; reserve A for rare named beats.

8. **Felt mood is affective congruence, not spatial presence and not mixer complexity.** Christoph Klimmt et al., 2018 (*Media Psychology* 22(5), DOI 10.1080/15213269.2018.1507827): music → enjoyment via emotion; spatial-presence and identification mediations *failed*. Hans-Peter Gasselseder, 2014 (Audio Mostly, DOI 10.1145/2636879.2636908): N=60, *Arkham City*; dynamic score raised imaginary/sensory immersion vs static (χ²(60)=6.23, p=.04), while sensory-spatial tracked *arousal potential*, not dynamism. **Implication:** a grim district wants a congruent held stem (C + slow B), not a busier mixer.

**If believed, keep AudioCommands as semantic cue IDs (C), execute stings as overlays (A), and treat intensify/soften as client RTPCs on a zone stem keyed to district tone (slow B).** Forbid Motif-in-engine, per-tick vertical remix, unquantized stem kills, and treating victory as a swap that stops the zone.
