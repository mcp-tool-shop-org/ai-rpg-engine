Fill the Godot stage first as the felt client. Presence is earned by a playable scene with a sensorimotor loop and spatial audio, not by beeping `run` or embedding a web player that autoplay-blocks.

1. **Presence is place illusion plus plausibility, both gated by sensorimotor contingencies and events that address the player—not by overlaying stings on a log.** Slater 2009 (https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2009.0138). Place illusion is constrained by what the system lets you look/move/act; plausibility by events that relate to *you*. A CLI beep is feedback, not being-there. Godot is the only listed consumer that can host both.

2. **Immersion’s medium presence effect is driven by tracking, stereo, and field of view—not by upgrading auditory content.** Cummings & Bailenson 2016 (https://doi.org/10.1080/15213269.2015.1015740). Across 115 effect sizes from 83 studies, visual-spatial display features beat quality of visual *and* auditory content. Terminal stings and a thin web embedder both miss the levers that actually move presence.

3. **The largest measured presence effects in games are display/controller and co-player nature, not “add music.”** Caroux 2023 (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apergo.2022.103936). HMD-plus-motion vs monitor-plus-classic control is a large effect on global presence; human vs computer co-players is very large on social presence. Filling Godot’s visual/action loop is the presence lever; filling `run` with CORE_SOUND_PACK is not.

4. **Music *does* move enjoyment—and almost nothing else in that meta-analysis does.** Caroux & Pujol 2024 (https://doi.org/10.1080/10447318.2023.2210880). Only music-present vs music-absent reached significance; difficulty and control mode did not. Treat terminal audio as an enjoyment garnish after a felt scene exists. Do not confuse “sounds nice” with “the world is felt.”

5. **Diegetic SFX in a visual game raise subjective immersion; tonic physiology often does not move.** Nacke, Grimshaw & Lindley 2010 (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intcom.2010.04.005). Sound on/off affected every Game Experience Questionnaire dimension; music had no main effect on EMG/EDA. FAIL: audio-as-physiology. Works: SFX as confirmation *inside* a visual action loop—the Godot case, not a TUI overlay.

6. **TUI sound is a known optional enhancement, not the presence channel; captions are the primary equivalent.** Ge 2014 (https://www.gridsagegames.com/blog/2014/04/sound-roguelikes/); Ge 2020 (https://www.gamedeveloper.com/audio/audio-accessibility-features-for-roguelikes); W3C WCAG 2.1 SC 1.2.2 (https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/captions-prerecorded.html). Most roguelikes skip SFX because variety plus instantaneous turns make audio chaotic. Cogmind’s win was 1,000+ SFX *plus* particles *plus* a caption-like audio log (off by default). WCAG Level A requires captions for audio, not audio for text. Terminal: log/captions first; SFX later.

7. **Web as first felt surface fails the first-hour audio test.** Beaufort 2018, Chrome autoplay (https://developer.chrome.com/blog/autoplay); Godot 4.7 export docs (https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/tutorials/export/exporting_for_web.html). Unmuted autoplay needs a gesture; AudioContext starts suspended. Godot’s default web **Sample** mode drops AudioEffects, reverb, procedural audio, and often positional audio. Native Godot buses let you toggle Playing and watch VU meters in-editor (https://docs.godotengine.org/en/latest/tutorials/audio/audio_buses.html)—the hear-it-in-the-editor loop web does not give.

8. **The discriminator is a human-played slice, not a demo reel; first sessions die in minutes if that slice is missing.** Ismail 2022 (https://ltpf.ramiismail.com/prototypes-and-vertical-slice/); Robinson 2016, deltaDNA ~5,000 games (https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/how-first-session-length-impacts-game-performance). Prototypes answer *should*; a vertical slice answers *can* at near-ship feel. 20% of F2P installs are gone in two minutes; first sessions under nine minutes average 20% D1 retention vs 31% above. Studio metric already matches this: one Godot scene a human has played.

If believed, this cycle fills Godot to that one playable scene with bus-routed stings/voice, and treats CLI as the onboarding path, not the felt world. It forbids shipping “felt” as terminal beeps, a Pages embed, or all three surfaces at once. Captions/log on `run` may follow; they do not earn presence.
